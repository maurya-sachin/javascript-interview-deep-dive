A name and mark you can actually own

"Deep Dive" is also the name of an interview round, so it is hard to find and hard to protect. Below are two distinctive options built on your four depth levels (30 s, answer, follow-ups, spec), plus keeping the current name. These are first drafts to choose a direction from, not final artwork.

isobath
isobath

isobath (recommended)

An isobath is the line on a map joining points of equal depth, so the mark is a contour map: four depth levels, lightest at the surface, deepest at the core. Rare word, so search results are yours and it is the strongest to protect. Spelling takes a moment to learn. isobath.dev showed no DNS record; .com and .io are taken.

plumb
plumb

plumb

To plumb is to measure depth, and plumb also means exactly vertical, as in true. That fits "know the answer, and why it is true". Friendly and short. It is a common English word, so it is a weaker trademark and harder to rank for, and plumb.dev and plumb.so are already registered.

Deep Dive

Keep "Deep Dive"

Continuity with the v1 site and its stars. But it is a generic phrase for an interview round, so searches for it return other people's pages, and a descriptive name is the hardest kind to trademark. It could stay as a tagline: "isobath: engineering interviews, in depth".

I dropped the stacked-bars mark from the earlier mockup. Three stacked bars is a very common "layers" icon, so it was too generic to own.
