C. Illustrated tracks

Friendly spot art per track, in the style of web.dev and Kaggle. Strongest at a glance for beginners. Needs new art for every topic (28 and growing) and dates fastest.

My lean: A, with one signature motif borrowed from B (the depth strata) instead of illustrations. Type and structure age well; spot art scales poorly past a few dozen topics.
